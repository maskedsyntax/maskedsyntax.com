# MaskedSyntax brand assets

The mark is a woven, modular monogram: one form passes behind another to express concealment without drawing a literal mask, while the folded pieces suggest multiple products growing from one identity. It is designed to stay readable at favicon/avatar sizes and to work as a one-color silhouette when needed.

## Palette

- Syntax cobalt: `#3B5BFF`
- Ink: `#0B0C10`
- Paper: `#F7F7F8`
- Muted: `#A8ADBA`

## Web assets

- `web/maskedsyntax-mark-adaptive.svg` — automatically follows the visitor's preferred color scheme
- `web/maskedsyntax-mark-light.svg` / `web/maskedsyntax-mark-dark.svg` — explicit theme variants
- `web/maskedsyntax-lockup-light.svg` / `web/maskedsyntax-lockup-dark.svg` — mark plus wordmark
- `web/maskedsyntax-mark-mono.svg` — one-color reproduction
- Matching 512 px transparent PNG exports are included for raster-only surfaces

Use the mark at 24 px or larger. Leave clear space around it equal to roughly one quarter of its width. Do not put the two-color light variant on a dark background or the dark variant on a light background.

## YouTube assets

- `youtube/maskedsyntax-youtube-avatar.png` — 1024 × 1024 PNG; safe for YouTube's circular crop
- `youtube/maskedsyntax-youtube-banner.png` — 2560 × 1440 PNG; meaningful content sits inside the centered 1546 × 423 cross-device safe area

The SVG sources are included beside each PNG for future edits.
